I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial Engineering at Tsinghua University, working under Associate Professor Dr. Hai Jiang. My current research focuses on deep reinforcement learning (DRL) and deep inverse reinforcement learning (DIRL), which are applied to optimize the ride-hailing experience in autonomous taxi systems. My research interests lie at the intersection of mathematical modeling, data-driven optimization, machine learning, data analysis, and operations research, with applications in transportation, environmental challenges, healthcare, and energy systems. This involves using mathematical modeling, optimization techniques, advanced machine learning, and deep learning methods to address transportation and mobility challenges. The goal is to create innovative solutions that improve transport efficiency and user experience.

======
I was awarded a Visiting Researcher fellowship by the Ministry of Education of the Slovak Republic under the SAIA program. I worked in the Department of Mathematical Methods and Operations Research at the University of Zilina (Slovakia) with Prof. Dr. Lubos Bunza on optimization models and solution algorithms for cross-border and inter-regional multimodal journey planning.
